A Lost Kingdom Awaits.

Return is a high-stakes, turn-based roguelike TCG where survival isn't just found in card strategy, but earned through mechanical precision. You play as Edrick, a fallen warrior navigating a shifting ancestral fortress where every card is a weapon fueled by blood. Unlike traditional card games, Return features a real-time "Parry-to-Play" loop: perfectly timed parries (a well-aimed left-click) grant you the stamina needed to unleash devastating counter-attacks. If you hesitate, you die; if you dominate, you evolve.

Core Features

Parry-to-Play Combat

A real-time parry timing system built right into a turn-based card game. Actively defend against incoming attacks using a dynamic precision meter to nullify damage and generate combat stamina. Perfect parries completely negate incoming damage and grant a substantial stamina boost to fuel your next turn.

Strategic Deck-Building

Build your active deck by choosing up to 5 strategic weapon, shield, or spell cards from your permanent cross-run inventory before entering combat. Every card is a weapon fueled by blood — heavy axes, swift bows, crushing fireballs, and blessed shields await your command.

Roguelite Progression

Death ends your run — your run-only cards are lost, but you may rescue a single collected card to keep permanently. Your level, experience, and spent attribute points always carry over, so every run you return stronger and eventually crush the castle's defenders. The kingdom remembers. Do you?

Deep Attribute RPG

Earn combat experience (XP) to level up and manually distribute points into five distinct core attributes. Strength scales physical attacks, Vigor expands your health bar, Intelligence enhances spell cards, Endurance expands your stamina, and Dexterity eases your parry timing.

The Story

Step into the heavy armor of Edrick, a once-respected knight of the kingdom who was unjustly accused of murdering the king's right hand and forcefully exiled. Now branded an outlaw, he returns with only a sword and shield, determined to claw his way up the ancestral castle to claim his vengeance.

The Twist: As you fight through your former allies, dark memories surface. Through mid-game flashbacks and dialogue, you discover the terrifying truth: the assassination was orchestrated by your own wife, who manipulated those closest to you to provoke your ruin. She now awaits you at the throne.

Gameplay Mechanics

The Precision Parry Meter

When an enemy strikes, a precision parry bar appears. Players must left-click at the exact right moment. The ideal parry target changes positions with every enemy attack, and its width scales dynamically with your current stamina.

Poor Parry Red Zone

Missed timing. Results in severe health loss and a massive drain on your stamina.

Normal Parry Yellow Zone

Mitigates most damage and yields a small stamina recovery.

Perfect Parry Green Zone

Completely negates incoming damage and grants a substantial stamina boost to fuel your next turn.

The RPG Attributes

Tailor Edrick to your personal playstyle by distributing experience points across five core attributes:

  • Strength

    Dynamically scales physical attacks and heavy weapon cards.

    Example: Battle Axe deals 22 base damage and scales ×1.4 with Strength — at 10 Strength it hits for 36. Strength also adds to your physical defense.

  • Vigor

    Permanently expands your maximum health bar.

    Example: With 0 Vigor your max HP is 80; each point adds 10, so 6 Vigor gives 140 HP. A larger pool also means your Health Potion (40% of max HP) restores more.

  • Intelligence

    Greatly enhances the damage and effects of magical spell cards.

    Example: Ravenwood Crook deals 12 base magic damage and scales ×1.2 with Intelligence — at 10 Intelligence it hits for 24. Intelligence also adds to your magic defense.

  • Endurance

    Expands your total stamina capacity.

    Example: With 0 Endurance your max stamina is 70; each point adds 15, so 2 Endurance gives 100 stamina. More stamina also keeps parry zones wider and the icon slower.

  • Dexterity

    Eases your parry timing and powers Dexterity-based weapons.

    Example: Hunter Bow scales ×1.1 with Dexterity, so it grows as you invest — and higher Dexterity slows the parry icon, widening your timing window.

The Shifting Floors

The Forest of Beginnings — Tutorial

A calm, mist-shrouded, steel-blue area designed to let players learn card mechanics, parrying, and movement basics before storming the gates. No enemies here pose a real threat; the goal is to master the Parry-to-Play loop in a safe environment.

The Mystic Courtyard & Entrance

Bathed in desaturated teals and eerie violet washes. Face off against Corrupted Knights, Pale Lancers, and Hooded Heralds.

Boss: Galahad of the Hidden Axe — a hulking executioner whose overhead cleaves demand precise parries to answer.

The Bloody Halls

A terrifying, dark environment stained in deep crimson. Contend with Bog Witches, Shield-maiden Wraiths, and Marauder Orcs.

Boss: Isolde, the Draconic Maiden — a hybrid threat who weaves physical strikes and searing magic, punishing a one-school defense.

The Throne of Return

A ruined royal space rendered in abyssal blacks and tarnished gold. Fight Crystal Gargoyles, Rotten Spirits, and Ancient Sentinels before the final confrontation.

Final — a two-stage fight: Eldric the Forlorn King awaits at the summit; when he falls, Lysara the Veiled Sorcerer takes the throne. Both must be defeated in a single attempt.

Art & Audio

Visual Philosophy: The game features a striking digital hand-painted watercolor-and-ink aesthetic. It leverages desaturated medieval tones mixed with stark, fine-liner analog lineart to make the entire screen feel like an organic, handcrafted adventurer's field journal.

UI Aesthetic: All menu elements, card templates, and battle overlays incorporate a distinct watercolor paper and parchment grain texture.

Sonic Atmosphere: Heavily inspired by the tense, unsettling, yet deeply melancholic polyphonies found in Elden Ring and Nier: Replicant. Combat audio clearly differentiates the sharp, metallic crunch of a successful parry from regular damage to ensure crisp tactical feedback.
